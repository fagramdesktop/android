## New
- Updated to Telegram 13.1.1 (rich quotes, gift deletion, wallet stuff, some bugfixes)
- [Appearance › Icon pack](tg://settings/inu/icon-replacement): install custom icon packs
- [Messages › Also hide replies to them](tg://settings/inu/blocked-messages-replies): option to hide replies to blocked users
- Privacy & Security › Paranoia mode: hide Telegram Stars and GRAM balance, transactions and stats
- Round messages use the new experimental stock Camera2 recorder by default
